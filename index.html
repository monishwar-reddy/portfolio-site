About Me

Bridging computational engineering, biomedical intelligence, and global youth leadership

Hi, I’m Monishwar Reddy Vardireddy, currently pursuing my B.Tech at Woxsen University, specializing in Computer Science.

I am deeply passionate about working at the intersection of technology, computational research, and sustainability. Over the years, I have explored a wide range of areas, including mathematical modeling, biomedical signal processing, and EEG-based human-machine interfaces.

My experience as a Research Assistant and Intern has allowed me to contribute to high-impact research projects involving SEIR epidemiology, artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), and myocardial infarction detection algorithms.

Through this program, I am advancing SDG 4: Quality Education through student-led initiatives.

I am proficient in Python, data structures, algorithms, and statistical modeling, with a focus on designing scalable and efficient computational solutions. I am passionate about developing robust software systems and applying machine learning to solve real-world problems. I have a strong foundation in software engineering, machine learning, and data-driven systems.
